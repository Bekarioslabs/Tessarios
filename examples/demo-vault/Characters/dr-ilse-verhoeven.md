## Character Core
A tidal scientist who is right about almost everything and cannot tolerate the one thing she cannot measure. She is not cruel; she is thorough, and thoroughness does not stop when it should.

## Public Persona & Appearance
Neat grey coat, a clipboard, a habit of apologising before she asks something intrusive.

## Goals & Motivation
To explain the bay's strange tides, officially, with numbers. If the explanation involves a child, she will treat the child as a data point.

## Methods & Resources
Survey permits, access to the [[Harbour Authority]], and the authority to close the pier "for safety".

## Knowledge of Events
Has noticed that every anomalous reading coincides with one schoolgirl on the pier.

## Weaknesses & Limits
Needs proof she can publish. A glowing puddle that nobody else sees is not proof.

## Open Questions
- Does she know about the lamp, or only about the pier?
