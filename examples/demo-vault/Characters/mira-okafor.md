## Character Core

Mira is curious to the point of trespassing. She asks the question everyone else has agreed not to ask, and she cannot leave a locked door alone. Underneath that, she is afraid of being the kind of person things happen *to* rather than someone who makes them happen.

## Everyday Life & Personality

Lives above her parents' bakery on Harbour Street. Sketches every odd thing she sees in a salt-stained notebook. Loud when she is sure, silent when she is not, and she hides which is which by talking faster.

## Discovery of Powers

At the lowest tide in thirty years she stands in a puddle beneath [[Saltmarsh Pier]] and the water starts to glow around her boots. It takes her two more visits to understand that the light follows *her*, not the tide.

## Powers & Limitations

Tidelight lets her draw a soft glow out of standing salt water. It needs the sea nearby, it tires her within minutes, and it is visible to anyone looking. She cannot aim it; it spreads where she is calm.

## Hero Identity & Costume

No costume yet. [[Jonas Brandt]] is building a rain cloak with reflective tape so that the glow reads as a trick of the light instead of a person.

## Role in Group

Founder and, reluctantly, leader of [[The Tidewatch Club]]. Pushes forward; relies on [[Jonas Brandt]] to say when it is a bad idea.

## Key Relationships

- [[Jonas Brandt]] — best friend, the only one who knows.
- [[Mr. Halvorsen]] — the old keeper, who watches her a little too closely.
- [[Dr. Ilse Verhoeven]] — surveying the bay; asks Mira the same polite questions three days running.

## Plot Role

Carries the main arc of [[The Lamp Mystery]]: from accidental discovery to choosing, deliberately, to light the lamp.

## Secrets
She has already been inside [[Old Lighthouse]] once, through the cellar, and has told nobody.
