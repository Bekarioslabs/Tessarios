## Character Core
Jonas needs to understand how a thing works before he can be afraid of it. He is careful where Mira is bold, and he keeps lists: of tide times, of what could go wrong, of what they have already tried.

## Everyday Life & Personality
Fixes bicycles for the street in exchange for biscuits. Walks with a screwdriver in his jacket pocket. Dry humour, delivered so flatly that people only laugh a moment later.

## Role in Group
The engineer and the brake. Builds what the club needs and says "no" to what it should not do, usually a second after Mira has already done it.

## Key Relationships
- [[Mira Okafor]] — trusts her completely and tells her so only in practical terms.

## Plot Role
Makes the [[Tidewatch Cloak]] and works out the tide pattern that points to the pier.
