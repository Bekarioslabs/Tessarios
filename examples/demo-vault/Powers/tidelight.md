## Overview
The ability to draw a soft, pale glow out of standing salt water.

## Origin & Creation
Connected, somehow, to [[The Night of the Long Tide]] and to [[The Brass Lamp]]. How is deliberately left open.

## Abilities
Makes puddles, rock pools and tidal flats glow. The light can be dimmed, not switched off.

## Limits & Costs
Needs salt water nearby. Tires the bearer within minutes. Cannot be aimed; spreads where the bearer is calm. Visible to everyone.

## First Manifestation
Under [[Saltmarsh Pier]], at the lowest tide in thirty years.
