## Overview
Regulates moorings, dredging and, since last spring, "tidal anomalies". Mostly harmless paperwork, until it is not.

## Goals & Interests
Keep the harbour open and insured. A mysterious tide is bad for both.

## Plot Role
Supplies [[Dr. Ilse Verhoeven]] with the legal power to close [[Saltmarsh Pier]].
