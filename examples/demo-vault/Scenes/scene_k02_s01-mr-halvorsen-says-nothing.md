## Manuscript
Mr. Halvorsen was polishing the brass doorplate when she arrived, which was odd, because the doorplate had been clean for thirty years.

"I found this under the pier," said Mira, and held out the bracket.

He did not take it. He did not even glance down. Mr. Halvorsen, who was fully informed about the lamp and had been for thirty years, kept his eyes on the doorplate and rubbed it in slow, careful circles.

"The pier is a dangerous place at low tide," he said.

"That's not an answer."

"No," said Mr. Halvorsen. "It is a weather report."
