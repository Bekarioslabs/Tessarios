## Overview & Premise
At the lowest tide in thirty years, eleven-year-old Mira discovers that she can make seawater glow. To learn why, she and her friend Jonas must get past a tidal scientist, a silent lighthouse keeper and a lamp that has not been lit since before they were born.

## Central Questions & Conflict
Who lit the lamp on [[The Night of the Long Tide]]? Can Mira trust the adults around her, or only Jonas?

## Character Focus
[[Mira Okafor]] carries the book. [[Jonas Brandt]] is her counterweight; [[Dr. Ilse Verhoeven]] is the pressure from outside.

## Story Arc
### Beginning
The glow under [[Saltmarsh Pier]].

### Middle
The trail to [[Old Lighthouse]] and [[The Brass Lamp]].

### Climax & Ending
The pier is closed; Mira lights the lamp on purpose.
