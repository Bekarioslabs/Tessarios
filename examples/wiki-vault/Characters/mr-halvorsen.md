## Character Core
Retired lighthouse keeper who never retired from the lighthouse. Speaks in short sentences and polishes things that are already clean.

## Everyday Life & Personality
Feeds the gulls on the lighthouse steps at seven sharp. Fond of exact times and of silence. Treats curiosity from children as weather: something to wait out.

## Relationship to Key Characters
Lets [[Mira Okafor]] borrow the lighthouse key for exactly one hour a week and never asks what for.

## Plot Role
Guardian of the [[The Brass Lamp]]. As far as the story has established, he believes it is only a museum piece.

## Knowledge of Events
Knows the official version of [[The Night of the Long Tide]]. Does not know about Tidelight.
