## Overview
The lighthouse lamp that burned through the whole of [[The Night of the Long Tide]]. One pane of the lens is cracked.

## Appearance & Composition
Brass housing gone dark green at the seams, a Fresnel lens the size of a wheelbarrow, a bracket stamped with a single letter H.

## Plot Role
Reacts, faintly, when [[Mira Okafor]] stands near it.
