## Overview
A collapsible energy blade from an early concept sheet: a "what if" gadget that the story does not use yet. It is in this vault to show how concept art becomes the cover image of an entry.

## Appearance & Composition
A compact, dark handle with gold trim. A star-shaped button sits at the top; pressing it extends a violet blade of plasma light. Light and balanced. The labels on the concept sheet are in German and left as drawn.

## Functions & Uses
Compact when idle, a blade when activated.

## Limits & Vulnerabilities
Not decided. A children's adventure may not want a weapon at all.
