## Overview
Thirty years ago the sea left Greywater Bay for a single night and came back at dawn. The official report calls it a seiche.

## Knowledge Within the World
Everyone over forty remembers it. Nobody agrees on what they saw.

## Secrets & Revelations
The lamp in [[Old Lighthouse]] was lit the whole time, and nobody remembers lighting it.

## Open Questions
- Was anyone on the sand flats that night?
