## The idea
What if the glow is not a power but a *timer*? Every time [[Mira Okafor]] makes the water shine, something under the bay counts down.

## Possibilities
- The lamp in the [[Old Lighthouse]] is the other half of the clock.
- [[Dr. Ilse Verhoeven]]'s tide measurements are the first sign that it is running.
- A gadget such as [[Star Blade]] would not fit this tone. Maybe a smaller, quieter object does.
