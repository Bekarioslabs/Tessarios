## Overview
[[Mira Okafor]] and [[Jonas Brandt]] watch the tide refuse to come back.

## Scene Function
Opens the book on a wrong note: something is off with the sea.

## Manuscript
The tide went out further than Mira had ever seen it. By six o'clock the water had pulled back past the third row of pilings, leaving the sand ribbed and shining, and the whole pier stood on its long wooden legs like something that had waded in and forgotten to wade out.

"It's not supposed to do that," said Jonas. He sat on an upturned crate with a tide table spread across his knees, holding it down against the wind with both elbows. "Low water was at five-forty. It should be coming back by now."

Mira didn't answer. She had stepped into a puddle without noticing, and now she was looking at it.

The water around her boots had begun to glow. A faint, pale light, swelling and fading as slowly as breathing.

"Jonas," she said, very carefully, "don't look at the tide table. Look at my feet."
