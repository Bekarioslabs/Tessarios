## Overview
They test the glow, then crawl under the pier and find the brass bracket.

## Continuity
[[Mira Okafor]] has not yet told anyone. [[Tidelight]] has not been named.

## Manuscript
They tried it four times. When Mira stood still, the light came; when she thought about it, it went. When she stopped thinking about it, it came back even brighter, and Jonas wrote *calm = good?* in the margin of the tide table and underlined it twice.

"We should tell someone," he said.

"No."

"Mira."

"If we tell someone, they'll close the pier."

Jonas looked at the light, then at her, then at the dark gap beneath the boards where the puddle ran away into the sand. He sighed the sigh of a boy who has already decided to crawl in after her.

Under the pier it smelled of salt and old rope. The glow ran ahead of them along the pilings, and at the sixth post it lit something that was not wood: a bracket of green-black brass, bolted into the timber, stamped with a single letter. An H.
