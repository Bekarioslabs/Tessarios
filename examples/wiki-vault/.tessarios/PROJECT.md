# Project Orientation — Tessarios Wiki

This vault is an example and a small wiki about the app Tessarios. The entries are examples from a
fictional children's adventure, *The Lamp Under the Pier*. The tip boxes and the notes explain the app.

Format: illustrated children's novel (the example project)
Target audience: 9–12

## Tone
- Adventure, friendship, a light mystery.
- Humour comes from character and dialogue, not slapstick.
- No graphic violence. Danger is real but resolved in an age-appropriate way.

## Narrative frame
- Close third person on one main character per chapter.
- Powers appear gradually and are never fully under control.
- The wider mythology is hinted at, not explained, unless the plot needs it.

## Working principle
The vault is the truth. New ideas are proposals until the author adopts them.
Text inside tip boxes (`> [!tip]`) and the notes tagged `wiki` is documentation of the app, not story.
