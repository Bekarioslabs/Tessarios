## Overview
A long wooden pier on tall pilings, half closed for repairs. Under it, at low tide, is where [[Mira Okafor]] first sees the glow.

## Location & Environment
Runs 200 metres out from the harbour wall. At low water the sand beneath it is ribbed and shining.

## Access
Open to walkers until dusk. The boathouse at the landward end is used by [[The Tidewatch Club]].

## Atmosphere
Creaking boards, gulls, the smell of tar and wet rope.

## Plot Role
The place where Tidelight first shows itself and where [[Dr. Ilse Verhoeven]] takes her readings.
