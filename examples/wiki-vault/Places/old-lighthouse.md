## Overview
Decommissioned thirty years ago and never quite abandoned. Home of [[Mr. Halvorsen]] and of [[The Brass Lamp]].

## Access
Front door locked at all times. The key is lent out, rarely.

## Layout & Areas
Cellar, keeper's kitchen, a spiral stair of 117 steps, the lamp room at the top.

## History
Built after a wreck in the bay; went dark the morning after [[The Night of the Long Tide]].

## Plot Role
Holds the answer to the lamp mystery and the one person who will not discuss it.
