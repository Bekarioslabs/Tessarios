## Overview
A small harbour town on a grey, shallow bay. Famous for fog, fish soup and a tide that has behaved oddly since [[The Night of the Long Tide]].

## Location & Environment
Wide sand flats at low water, a crescent of old warehouses, and the long wooden finger of [[Saltmarsh Pier]].
