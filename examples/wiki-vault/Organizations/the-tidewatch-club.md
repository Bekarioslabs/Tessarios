## Overview
Two friends, a borrowed boathouse and a notebook of tide times. Officially a school project on coastal wildlife.

## Goals & Interests
Find out why the tide behaves strangely near the pier, and keep it from the grown-ups for as long as possible.

## Members & Roles

- [[Jonas Brandt]] — engineer, record keeper.

## Headquarters & Resources
