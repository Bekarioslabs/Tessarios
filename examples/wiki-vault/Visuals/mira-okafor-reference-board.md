## Visual Goal
One look at the character sheet should tell you who [[Mira Okafor]] is: curious, quick, a little untidy.

## Mandatory Features
Round purple glasses. A long braid. A navy hoodie with a small star on the sleeve. A crossbody satchel with a magnifying-glass emblem.

## Form & Silhouette
Small, slight, always mid-movement. The braid and the satchel make the silhouette readable from behind.

## Colors & Materials
Navy and dark purple, accents in gold and teal. Soft cotton, worn at the cuffs.

## Costume Parts & Details
Hoodie, dark leggings with a star at the ankle, trainers in navy and purple, the satchel.

## Variable Features
Expression, the position of the satchel, whether the hood is up.

## Reference Images
Two pictures sit in the gallery at the top: the character sheet and the item concept for [[Star Blade]].

## Base Prompt
Character sheet of an eleven-year-old girl with a long braid and round purple glasses, navy hoodie, dark leggings, trainers, crossbody satchel; neutral grey background; front, back and side views plus facial expressions.

## Negative / Avoid
Text on clothing, photographic skin, adult proportions.

## Prompt Tests
*Example text, invented for this wiki:* Test 1 gave good proportions but no braid. Test 2 added "long braid over the shoulder" and worked.

## Current Production Prompt
The base prompt with "long braid over the shoulder".

## Known Issues
The star on the sleeve is often missing or too large.

## Production Notes
Tessarios does not generate images; this file is where the prompts and decisions live.
