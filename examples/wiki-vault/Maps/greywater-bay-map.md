> [!tip] What is a map entry? (pins)
> A map holds an image and pins on it. Each pin can link to another entry, so the map becomes a way to move around the world.
>
> **What you can do:** Open the map editor, add pins, drag them, give them a colour, an icon and a description. The legend table below is written by the app from your pins.
>
> **Connects to:** A pin can point to a place, character, organization or any other entry; click it to jump there.
>
> **Try this:** Open the map and click the three pins. The second map in this vault shows regions and floors.

## Overview
Schematic map of the bay with the three places that matter: [[Saltmarsh Pier]], [[Old Lighthouse]] and [[Harbour Authority Office]].
