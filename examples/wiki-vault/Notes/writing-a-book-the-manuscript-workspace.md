## Content
The Manuscript workspace shows your writing as **Book → Chapter → Scene**. A chapter needs a book, a scene needs a chapter, so the tree stays meaningful. Each scene and chapter has a status and an automatic word count that rolls up to the book.

A scene entry holds both planning (function, goal, conflict, continuity) and, in the last section called **Manuscript**, the story text. Only that section is meant to be the story.

**The right column has two tabs.** *Dossier* lets you look something up while you write. *AI* works on the open scene, wearing one of two hats:
- **Writing partner** proposes prose. You choose **Append** (adds to the end, nothing can be lost) or **Replace selection** (writes over exactly the passage you marked; one undo takes it back). No button overwrites a whole scene.
- **Editor** only comments. It has no apply buttons, so you cannot paste a critique into your story by accident.

The AI sees the scene, its chapter and book, how the previous scene ends, and the dossier of every entry the scene links with `[[...]]`.
