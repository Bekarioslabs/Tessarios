## Content
**Tessarios is finished.** This is the final version: no updates, no bug fixes, no support. The source is public under the AGPL (version 3 or later) so anyone can use, study and continue it. Forks are welcome; please give a fork a different name and logo.

**Limitations**
- Developed and tested on Windows only. Builds are not code-signed, so Windows may warn.
- No update check. The Chromium inside Electron stays at the version of the build.
- The AI features are an early beta, to be used with care; the app is a complete tool without them.
- German and English interface only. Files are Obsidian-compatible, but Obsidian plugins and Canvas are not supported.
- Keep your own backups.

**Images in this vault** were generated by the author with ChatGPT and Gemini. They are examples, not part of the program, and they are not covered by the AGPL.

**Support the creator.** Tessarios is free. If you want to say thanks, you can leave a [voluntary tip](https://buy.stripe.com/cNicN7b8KejV4Z39WtbjW00). It is not needed to download or use the app, and nothing is given in return: no products, services, updates or support.

**Where to look:** the README has the download and the source. `ARCHITECTURE.md` explains how the app is built, `FUTURE_IDEAS.md` lists ideas that were never built.
