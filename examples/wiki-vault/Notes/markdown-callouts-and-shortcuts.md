## Content
**Shortcuts in the editor:** `Ctrl+B` bold, `Ctrl+I` italic, `Ctrl+K` link the selection, `Ctrl+Shift+H` highlight, `Ctrl+Shift+X` strikethrough, `Ctrl+Shift+C` inline code, `Ctrl+Z` / `Ctrl+Shift+Z` undo and redo. Type `/` for the quick-insert menu (tables, callouts, checklists).

**Syntax:** `[[Title]]` link, `[[Title|text]]` link with other text, `![[image.png]]` embed. Tables, checklists (`- [ ]` and `- [x]`) and code blocks work. There is no math rendering.

**Callout boxes** (like the tip at the top of every example) are `> [!note]`, `> [!warning]`, `> [!tip]`, `> [!quote]`, `> [!bug]` and `> [!example]`, followed by a title and quoted lines.
