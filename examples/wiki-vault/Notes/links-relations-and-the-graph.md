## Content
Type `[[` in a field or a section and start typing a title: Tessarios offers the entries it knows, or lets you create a new one on the spot. **Auto-Detect WikiLinks** scans your text for names that match entries and offers to link them.

- `[[Title]]` links to an entry.
- `[[Title|shown text]]` links but shows different words.
- `![[assets/Map 01.jpg]]` embeds an image.

You do not have to maintain a separate list of relations. The links in your fields and your text *are* the relations.

**The Graph view** draws every entry as a node and every link as a line. Drag nodes around, filter by type, click a node to open the entry. Entries get a colour by type (the same colours as the chips in the sidebar).

**Try this:** open the Graph view and find [[Mira Okafor]]. Everything she is linked to is one line away, and so are the entries that link to her.
