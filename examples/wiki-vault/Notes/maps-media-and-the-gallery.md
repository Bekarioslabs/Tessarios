## Content
**Maps.** A map entry holds an image with pins, regions and optional floors. Pins and regions can link to any entry; click one to jump there. See [[Greywater Bay Map]] (pins) and [[Saltmarsh Pier Harbour Plan]] (regions and floors). Both images are in `assets/`.

**Cover image and gallery.** An entry can have a cover image and a gallery. [[Mira Okafor]] has a cover, [[Mira Okafor – Reference Board]] has a gallery.

**The Media view** lists every image and groups it by the entries that actually link to it. An image can belong to several entries, or to none yet (*unlinked*).

**Try this:** *Character sheet 02* in `assets/` is deliberately not linked to anything. Open the Media view, find it under unlinked, and link it to [[Jonas Brandt]]. It appears on his page afterwards.

**Compression.** Settings → General → *Compress Images on Import* shrinks large pictures when you add them (on by default). Switch it off to keep the originals.

**Vault Stats** shows word and character counts, a breakdown by category and recent activity across the whole vault.
