# Tessarios: ideas for forks

Tessarios is finished, so nothing here is planned or promised. This is a list of things that
came up and were never built, for anyone who forks the project. Some early ideas have since
been built (the manuscript workspace with chapters and scenes, map regions and floors,
version backups, a basic consistency check for scenes) and are no longer listed.

How the app is put together: [`ARCHITECTURE.md`](ARCHITECTURE.md). What the AI does and does
not do: [`docs/ki-spezifikation.md`](docs/ki-spezifikation.md). Coding rules:
[`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).

## Maps
* Thematic layers (politics, climate, trade routes) that can be toggled on one map. Today a map
  has floors, not layers.
* A scale and distance tool: set the canvas scale (for example 100 px = 50 km) and measure
  between pins.
* A fog-of-war mode that hides unexplored regions.

## Timelines and family trees
* A global timeline with eras, ages and wars, and dates on entries that fill it automatically.
* Side-by-side timelines of several characters.
* Family trees and bloodlines, and a relationship matrix between characters.

## Writing
* Point-of-view and location tags on scenes, and word-count goals per chapter.
* A character arc tracker across chapters.

## Export and import
* A read-only reader mode for sharing a world with readers or players.
* EPUB and PDF export of chosen entries, chapters and maps.
* Import from Obsidian or Notion beyond opening the folder as a vault.

## AI
* Prompt presets for names, heraldry, magic systems, flora and fauna.
* A name generator that follows the language style of a culture.
